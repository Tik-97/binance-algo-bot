- Where the strongest separation shows up: no single side clearly dominates.
- The most useful features in this sample are: Relative volume 5m, RSI 15m, Pre-entry return 15m %, Abs distance to EMA20 5m %.
- Side symmetry is limited: Long and Short do not separate in exactly the same way.
